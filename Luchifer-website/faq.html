01 — FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask before ordering.

These answers explain how Luchifer approaches custom uniform requirements. Product-specific commercial terms are confirmed in the quotation.

Do you make completely custom uniforms?
Yes. Product choice, fabric, colour, fit, branding, trims, size range and department combinations can be discussed around the requirement.
Can I send a reference image or existing garment?
Yes. A reference image, sketch or existing garment can help communicate the intended fit, colour, construction and branding direction.
Can different departments have different uniforms?
Yes. A single organisation can use different garments, colours or details for front office, service, kitchen, housekeeping, operations or other departments while keeping one brand system.
Do you provide samples?
Sample development can be discussed before bulk production. The exact sample process, cost and timeline depend on the product and requirement and are confirmed in the quotation.
How is pricing decided?
Custom pricing depends on the product, fabric, construction, branding method, quantity, size mix and delivery requirement. The final commercial quote is prepared after the requirement is understood.
Can I reorder later?
Yes, reorders can be discussed using the previously approved product details so the new requirement can follow the same specification as closely as practical.
Can you add our logo and employee details?
Logo embroidery, printing, labels and other identification details can be discussed. Names or role-wise identification depend on the product and production method.
Do you provide delivery across India?
Delivery requirements can be discussed for destinations across India. The quotation should confirm the destination, expected timeline and applicable delivery terms.
What about protective workwear?
For industrial requirements, the garment specification should be based on the actual hazard and required certified performance. Any applicable standard or certification must be specified and confirmed before production.
Can I request a complete uniform programme?
Yes. You can send the requirement for multiple departments or multiple garment types together, and the programme can be planned as one coordinated requirement.
