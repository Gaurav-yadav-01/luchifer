Luchifer Website — Research Upgrade Notes

This version keeps the existing Luchifer visual direction and adds the
information architecture observed across the reference websites supplied
by the owner.

Research themes incorporated:
- Fortera Apparel: clear custom-order workflow, artwork/mockup approval,
  product/industry organization, FAQs, sample/production explanation, and
  detailed product information.
- Vihaan International: manufacturing capabilities, industry-specific
  divisions, quality-control stages, export/delivery information,
  quote-oriented calls to action, and structured resource/footer navigation.
- Mafatlal Uniforms: strong category segmentation, institutional/industry
  collections, product catalogue structure, brand/trust presentation, and
  clear company/resource navigation.
- BR MODE: B2B-first positioning, industry/category pathways, bulk enquiry
  journeys, manufacturing/OEM framing, catalogue/resource access, and
  capability snapshots.
- Velocity Wear: quote form structure, MOQ/quantity discussion,
  material/decoration explanations, industry pages, FAQ/answer library,
  sample-before-bulk concept, reorder considerations, and
  delivery/production steps.

Luchifer-specific implementation:
- Home page visual design remains the existing Luchifer design.
- Existing product categories and unique-image rule are preserved;
  no image source was intentionally duplicated.

- Added FAQ page.

  terms; order-specific terms are confirmed in the quotation/order

- Products page links to the new information resources.
- Footer includes a Resources area.
- All local page references were validated; no missing local links found.
- JavaScript syntax was validated with Node.
- Image check: image sources remain unique across the HTML set.

Recent structural updates:
- The Chef Uniform page was merged into the Café & Restaurant page,
  because chef clothing is a core part of café/restaurant operations.
  The Café & Restaurant page now covers the complete team:
  waiters, chefs, reception, bar, kitchen support and housekeeping.
- A new School Uniform page was created (schools are a distinct market
  with their own clothing requirements: daily uniforms, sports wear,
  winter wear, house-wise sets and school staff uniforms).
- All navigation, footer links and product grids across every page were
  updated to reflect this change (Chef → School).

Important:
- Reference-site wording was not copied verbatim. The Luchifer copy is
  written specifically for Luchifer.
- Where the reference sites state their own prices, MOQs, certifications,
  production capacities, or policies, those figures were not transferred
  to Luchifer unless already part of Luchifer's own site information.
