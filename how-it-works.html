01 — How It Works

Clear from first brief to final delivery.

Our workflow is designed around custom requirements rather than forcing every organisation into one catalogue specification.

01 / BRIEF

Send the requirement

Tell us the product, industry, approximate quantity, sizes, colours, branding, reference images and required date.

02 / DISCUSS

Develop the option

We discuss fabric, construction, fit, branding method, department requirements and the practical use of the garment.

03 / APPROVE

Confirm the details

The agreed product specification, commercial terms and production requirement are confirmed before production.

04 / PRODUCE

Make & check

Production follows the approved requirement, with quality checks covering construction, branding and final presentation.

02 — What To Send

A better brief gives you a better quote.

For a faster response, include as many of these details as you already know. You can also simply send a reference image or an existing garment.

PRODUCT

What are you making?

Shirt, polo, blazer, chef coat, apron, coverall, jacket, trousers or a complete uniform programme.

QUANTITY

How many?

Approximate quantity and, if available, size or department breakdown.

IDENTITY

How should it look?

Brand colours, logo, embroidery or print preference, reference images and existing uniform examples.

USE

Where will it be worn?

Office, restaurant, hotel, kitchen, hospital, factory, delivery route, event or another working environment.

DATE

When do you need it?

Share the required date and destination so the production and delivery discussion is practical.

CONTACT

Who should we speak to?

Name, company, WhatsApp number and email if available.
